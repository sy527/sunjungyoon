Ph.D. Candidate · Health Services Research

SunJung Yoon, MS

Department of Health Policy & Management · University of Maryland, College Park · Expected May 2027

I study how payment structures, policy design, and institutional incentives shape physician behavior and patient outcomes — with a particular focus on the opioid epidemic, substance use disorder, and substance use screening in clinical settings. My work sits at the intersection of health economics and health policy, using large administrative datasets to understand the forces that drive prescribing behavior and access to care. I am also broadly interested in mental health policy, health disparities, and the economics of addiction.
